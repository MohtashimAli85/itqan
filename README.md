# Itqan

Itqan (إتقان, "doing your work with excellence") is an open-source desktop companion that lives on your screen as a small animated orb. It keeps your tasks and reminders, notices when you drift, pushes you toward the skills and projects you care about, looks after your health, and makes progress feel rewarding.

Local-first, bring your own AI key, no accounts, no telemetry.

## Status

Early planning. Nothing is runnable yet. macOS first, Windows and Linux by design.

See [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md) for the full plan.
